Paris, France

⭐ 4.8 (2,450 reviews)

Overview

Paris, the capital of France, is one of the most beautiful and romantic cities in the world. Known as the "City of Light," Paris has been a major center of art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture for centuries. With its iconic monuments, world-class museums, charming cafés, and exquisite cuisine, Paris offers an unforgettable travel experience.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April-May)

Mild weather, blooming flowers, and perfect temperatures. Great for sightseeing.

Fall (September-October)

Pleasant weather, fewer crowds, and beautiful autumn colors.

Must-See Attractions

🗼

Eiffel Tower

The iconic iron lattice tower and symbol of Paris. Stunning views of the city from the top.

🎨

Louvre Museum

World's largest art museum with masterpieces including the Mona Lisa.

⛪

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Magnificent Gothic architecture and historic cathedral on Île de la Cité.
